# Style Guide

## Tone
- Absurd but consistent: every strange rule must feel inevitable.
- Light, witty, and strange; avoid grimdark.
- Let humor come from logic taken too far, not randomness.

## Aesthetic Notes
- Mix craftsmanship with surreal bureaucracy.
- Keep technology tactile: brass, ink, lacquer, thread, glass.
- Avoid modern references (phones, cars, internet).

## Faction Voice Hints
- Ember Court: declarative, theatrical, hot metaphors.
- Verdant Assembly: patient, cyclical, growth language.
- Iron Pact: precise, mechanical, contractual.
- Rift Guild: elliptical, spatial, mirroring.
- Clockwork Chorus: rhythmic, formal, time-based phrasing.
- Gilt Caravan: persuasive, transactional, charming.

## Naming Reminders
- Names should sound like a title or trade.
- Two words is ideal, three acceptable, four only if compact.
- Avoid nonsense syllables or random concatenations.

## Text Discipline
- Card text should be short and unambiguous.
- Avoid forbidden content from canon taboos.
- Keep keywords consistent across cards.
