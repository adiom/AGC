# Absurd TCG Canon

## Core Premise
The world is called the Looping Expanse. Reality is semi-circular: events repeat until the absurd rule behind them is understood. The rule is always logical, but never conventional. Every faction exploits one rule and ignores another, which is why their conflicts are both inevitable and polite.

## Factions (6)

1) Ember Court
- Aesthetic: ceremonial ash, red lacquer armor, brass instruments, smoky parades.
- Mechanical signature: burst damage, "blaze" triggers on play, short-lived power spikes.
- Conflict: feuds with the Verdant Assembly over controlled burns that never end.

2) Verdant Assembly
- Aesthetic: tangled vines, moss cloaks, bark masks, gentle giants.
- Mechanical signature: growth counters, regeneration, long turns.
- Conflict: opposes the Iron Pact who harvest forests into machines.

3) Iron Pact
- Aesthetic: riveted steel, ticking engines, stamped insignias, factory hymns.
- Mechanical signature: artifacts, armor, damage reduction, steady tempo.
- Conflict: wants to mechanize the Rift Guild's portals for mass logistics.

4) Rift Guild
- Aesthetic: fractured glass, mirrored cloaks, folding geometry, whispered maps.
- Mechanical signature: repositioning, blinking, delayed effects.
- Conflict: sabotages the Clockwork Chorus because fixed timing offends them.

5) Clockwork Chorus
- Aesthetic: clock faces, pendulums, paper music rolls, polite uniforms.
- Mechanical signature: turn-based triggers, symmetry bonuses, extra phases.
- Conflict: exasperated by the Ember Court's improvisation.

6) Gilt Caravan
- Aesthetic: velvet tents, gilded wagons, lanterns, barter tokens.
- Mechanical signature: resource swaps, bargains, temporary control.
- Conflict: rivals the Iron Pact over trade routes and tariffs.

## Tabs of the Loop (World Rules)
- If a promise is written, it must be kept exactly as phrased.
- A name can be changed only once, and the old name becomes a spell.
- Every machine has a dream; dreams can power engines for one turn.
- A river can flow backward for a day if its stones are rearranged in order.
- Maps are considered living documents and can disagree with each other.

## Taboos (do not generate)
- Graphic violence or torture.
- Hate speech, real-world discrimination, or slurs.
- Sexual content or nudity (NSFW).
- Real-world politics, current conflicts, or identifiable public figures.
- Self-harm, suicide, or encouragement of harm.
- Extreme cruelty to animals or children.

## Naming Guide
- Use a concrete noun + an odd job or action: "Baker of Storms", "Latch Tinker".
- Combine a mundane object with a grand title: "Spool Regent", "Kettle Marshal".
- Prefer alliteration or internal rhyme, but keep it pronounceable.
- Avoid random word salad: 2-4 words, 10-30 characters total.
- If using a place, keep it short: "Rift Market", "Ash Stage".
